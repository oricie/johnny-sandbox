## פוסטים כתובים

**"Decided, but not in the product"**
> The most common thing I find in a product team's Slack isn't a bug.
>
> It's a decision. Made in a thread on a Tuesday, agreed by everyone, never built.
>
> On my first day with one team: "Severity shows as colour + word, everywhere" (decided 26 Aug). The queue was still grey text. 6 weeks later.

**"I checked my own work"**
> I wrote "no collapse control, every alert stays visible."
>
> Then I looked at the screenshot of what I'd built. There was a "Show 5 alerts" button.
>
> Now every build ends with me comparing the screen to what I said it does. If they don't match, I say so first.
