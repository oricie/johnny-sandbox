His first hour on your team

No new tool to log into and nothing to set up. You add him to a channel, and he gets to work.

  1. minute 0

    You add Johnny to a channel

    He introduces himself and starts reading the last 90 days.

  2. minute 1

    He connects what's been said

    Support threads, product decisions, customer calls and tickets, each traced to the screen it's about.

  3. minute 2

    He checks it against the product

    A decision from the chat counts as shipped only if he finds it in the code. If he doesn't, he says so.

  4. minute 3

    He tells you what he found

    What keeps coming back, what was decided but never shipped, and where a decision is needed. Then he asks what to sketch.

Then he does the work

A sketch on your real screen in about 30 seconds. When you say build, two options in their own branches, with live links, built from your own components and checked against what he said they'd do.
