title: Usability test, alert queue triage (5 analysts)
date: 2026-09-24
---
Task: "Find the most urgent alert and open it." Five analysts from three customers, remote, think-aloud.

- 4 of 5 scanned the queue top to bottom by time. Median 74 seconds to find the critical alert.
- 3 of 5 did not look at the Severity column at all until prompted. P3: "Oh, there's a severity column? It's all the same grey text."
- 2 of 5 opened two duplicate PowerShell alerts before realising they were the same. P5: "I wish these were one row with a count."
- 1 of 5 (P2) asked for an asset column: "I need to know if it's a test box or production before anything else."
- On the incident screen, 4 of 5 hovered the disabled Isolate button; only 1 noticed the tooltip. P4: "Why is it grey? Is it broken?"
